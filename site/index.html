Clinical Decision Support Assistant based on RAG

Capstone Project · University of Puerto Rico, Mayagüez · Fall 2026

Project Overview

A web-based clinical decision support assistant that helps healthcare professionals find and understand medical information using retrieval-augmented generation (RAG). Users submit clinical questions in natural language and receive concise answers based on trusted medical sources, with citations linking each statement to supporting evidence. The system verifies citations and supporting evidence before presenting responses and communicates when sufficient evidence is unavailable, helping clinicians make informed decisions without replacing professional medical judgment.
