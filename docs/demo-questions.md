# Demo Questions

## Answerable Questions

1. What is the first-line treatment for uncomplicated iron deficiency anemia in women?
2. What reticulocyte hemoglobin (CHr) cut-off was found to be optimal for detecting iron deficiency anemia?
3. What percentage of colorectal cancer patients had a formal diagnosis of iron deficiency anemia in their medical records?
4. In the trial comparing ferric carboxymaltose with iron sucrose in pregnant women, which group had the larger hemoglobin rise at 12 weeks, and by how much did each group rise?
5. In the meta-analysis comparing iron isomaltoside with iron sucrose, was the change in hemoglobin significantly different, and how many trials were included?

## Not answerable

6. What is the recommended antibiotic treatment for pneumonia in adults?
7. What is a heart attack?
